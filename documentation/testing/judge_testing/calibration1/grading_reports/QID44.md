# Question ID: 44
**Question:**  
How do you use Auto Hold after stopping the vehicle?

---

## Version V0

### V0 Answer
To use the Auto Hold feature in your 2022 Ford Escape after stopping the vehicle, follow these steps:

1. **Bring your vehicle to a complete stop.**  
   The Auto Hold active indicator will illuminate in the information display.

2. **Release the brake pedal.**  
   The system will hold your vehicle at a standstill, and the Auto Hold active indicator will remain illuminated.

3. **To move again, pull away in the normal manner.**  
   The system will release the brakes automatically, and the Auto Hold active indicator will turn off.

**Important Notes:**  
- The Auto Hold feature activates only if you apply sufficient brake pressure.  
- If you shift into reverse or if certain conditions are met (like the driver door being open), the system will deactivate.

### V1 Answer
To use the Auto Hold feature after stopping your vehicle, follow these steps:

1. **Bring the Vehicle to a Complete Stop**: Ensure your vehicle is fully stopped. The Auto Hold active indicator will illuminate in the information display.

2. **Release the Brake Pedal**: Once the vehicle is stopped, you can release the brake pedal. The Auto Hold system will maintain the brake pressure, keeping the vehicle stationary.

3. **Pull Away Normally**: When you're ready to move, simply press the accelerator pedal. The system will release the brakes automatically, and the Auto Hold active indicator will turn off.

### Important Notes:
- The Auto Hold feature activates only if you apply sufficient brake pressure.  
- If you shift into reverse (R) while the system is active, it will turn off.  
- The system may also apply the electric parking brake under certain conditions.
