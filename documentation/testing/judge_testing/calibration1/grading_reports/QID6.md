# Question ID: 6
**Question:**  
What does the seatbelt reminder indicator do?

---

## Version V0

### V0 Answer
The seatbelt reminder indicator in the 2022 Ford Escape serves several important functions:

1. **Initial Warning**: The indicator illuminates when you switch on the ignition if the driver’s seatbelt is not fastened. It will turn off once the seatbelt is fastened or after about one minute.

2. **Speed-Related Warnings**: If the vehicle speed exceeds 6 mph (10 km/h) and either the driver or front passenger has not fastened their seatbelt, the indicator will illuminate again, accompanied by an audible warning.

3. **Rear Seatbelt Monitoring**: The indicator will also alert you if a rear seatbelt is unfastened while the vehicle is in motion.

4. **Status Display**: The indicator can show the status of all seatbelts, indicating which are fastened or unfastened.

This system is designed to enhance safety by reminding occupants to buckle up.
