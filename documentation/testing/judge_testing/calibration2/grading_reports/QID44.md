# Question ID: 44
**Question:**  
How do you use Auto Hold after stopping the vehicle?

## Version V0

### V0 Answer
To use the Auto Hold feature in your 2022 Ford Escape after stopping the vehicle, follow these steps:

1. **Bring Your Vehicle to a Complete Stop**: Ensure the vehicle is fully stopped. The Auto Hold active indicator will illuminate in the information display.

2. **Release the Brake Pedal**: Once stopped, you can release the brake pedal. The system will hold your vehicle at a standstill, and the Auto Hold active indicator will remain illuminated.

3. **Pull Away Normally**: When you're ready to move, simply press the accelerator pedal. The system will release the brakes, and the Auto Hold active indicator will turn off.

### Important Notes:
- The Auto Hold feature activates only if you apply sufficient brake pressure.
- If you shift into reverse (R) while the system is active, it will turn off.
- The system may also apply the electric parking brake under certain conditions.
