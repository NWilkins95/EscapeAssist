# Question ID: 48
**Question:**  
What are the oil change interval differences between normal, severe, and extreme driving conditions?

## Version V0

### V0 Answer
The oil change intervals for the 2022 Ford Escape vary based on driving conditions:

1. **Normal Driving Conditions**:
   - Interval: **7,500 to 10,000 miles** (12,000 to 16,000 km)
   - Example: Normal commuting with highway driving, no or moderate load, flat to moderately hilly roads, no extended idling.

2. **Severe Driving Conditions**:
   - Interval: **5,000 to 7,500 miles** (8,000 to 12,000 km)
   - Example: Moderate to heavy load or towing, mountainous or off-road conditions, extended idling, extended hot or cold operation.

3. **Extreme Driving Conditions**:
   - Interval: **3,000 to 5,000 miles** (5,000 to 8,000 km)
   - Example: Maximum load or towing, extreme hot or cold operation, extended engine idling.
