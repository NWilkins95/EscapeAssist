# Question ID: 15
**Question:**  
Why is it important to use the correct engine oil specification?

## Version V0

### V0 Answer
Using the correct engine oil specification is crucial for several reasons:

1. **Component Protection**: Using oil that meets the specified standards helps protect engine components from wear and damage. Incorrect oil can lead to issues that may not be covered by your vehicle warranty.

2. **Performance**: The right oil ensures optimal engine performance. Using the wrong oil can result in longer engine cranking periods and reduced overall performance.

3. **Emissions Control**: Correct oil specifications help maintain emissions levels within regulatory standards. Incorrect oil can lead to increased emissions.

4. **Fuel Economy**: The right oil can improve fuel efficiency. Using oil that does not meet specifications may reduce fuel economy.

5. **Warranty Compliance**: Using the specified oil is often a requirement for maintaining your vehicle's warranty. Failure to comply can void warranty coverage for related damages. 

In summary, adhering to the correct engine oil specifications is essential for the longevity, efficiency, and reliability of your vehicle.
